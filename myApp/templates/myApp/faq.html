Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about AlphaMart

🛒 Buying
How do I buy an item?
Browse products, click on an item, and click "Add to Cart". Follow the checkout process to complete your purchase. All sellers are verified for your safety.
Is it safe to buy on AlphaMart?
Yes! All users are verified with email, phone number, and address. Sellers must upload real photos. We also have a complaint system if any issues arise.
Can I return an item?
Returns are handled between buyers and sellers. Discuss condition and returns before purchasing. For disputes, use our complaint system and we'll help resolve the issue.
💰 Selling
How do I sell my item?
Click "Sell" in the menu. Upload photos, add a title, description, price, and category. Once listed, buyers can contact you through our system.
Are there any fees for selling?
No! Listing items on AlphaMart is completely free. No listing fees, no commissions. You keep 100% of the sale price.
How many photos do I need?
Sellers must upload at least 1 photo per listing. More photos means more trust from buyers!
🔐 Account & Security
How do I create an account?
Click "Join Free" in the navigation. Provide your email, full name, phone number, and address. We verify all info to keep the community safe.
Is my personal information safe?
Yes! Your full details are never shared publicly. You control what information is visible through your profile settings.
⚖️ Payments & Disputes
What if I have a problem with a transaction?
Go to the "Report Issue" page and submit a complaint. Include details about the problem. Our team will review and help mediate within 48 hours.
Can I cancel an order?
Go to your Dashboard, find the order, and contact the seller. If the item hasn't been handed over yet, cancellation is possible by mutual agreement.
